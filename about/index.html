Hands-on background

Why the range matters

Better questions before the work is covered up.

A finish selection can affect framing. A layout change can affect plumbing, electrical, heating, and drywall. A basement plan can affect egress, moisture, storage, and access to mechanical equipment.

Understanding those connections helps identify risk earlier, coordinate the sequence more usefully, and explain trade-offs in terms a homeowner can act on.
