Privacy

How Enquiry Information Is Used

This page preserves the intent of Bellarosa’s current privacy statement and identifies the controls still needed before the new form is connected.

Website enquiries

Bella Rosa Developments Ltd. may collect information you choose to provide through this website so it can respond to your enquiry and understand interest in its services.

Information submitted for a project enquiry should be used for Bellarosa’s own follow-up and site analysis. It should not be sold to third parties.

Prototype status

The contact form in this speculative build does not transmit or store personal information. An owner-approved destination, retention policy, spam-protection method, and access controls must be configured and tested before launch.

Information you provide

A production enquiry form may collect your name, phone number, email address, project location, project type, optional budget and timeline information, and the message you choose to submit.

Owner verification required

Before publication, Bellarosa must approve the final privacy wording, identify who receives enquiries, define how long enquiry data is retained, and provide a contact for privacy questions.
