Privacy

How Contact Information Is Handled

This website does not collect, submit, or store project-enquiry information. Contact begins through your own email application or phone.

No website form or enquiry database

The website does not provide an online enquiry form, submit contact information to a server endpoint, or write enquiry content to a website database. It does not use an SMTP form-delivery service.

Direct email and phone contact

Email links open your device’s email application. Nothing is sent until you review the message and choose Send. Telephone links begin a call through your device. Once you contact Bella Rosa, the communication is handled by your provider and Bella Rosa’s normal email or telephone systems.

Use and retention

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to the enquiry, understand the proposed project, arrange follow-up, and maintain ordinary business correspondence. The website itself has no copy to retain or delete. Retention within email and telephone systems is governed by the relevant providers and Bella Rosa’s business record practices.

What not to send

Avoid including payment information, government identifiers, passwords, alarm details, lock codes, or other sensitive access information in an initial email.

Privacy questions

For questions about information sent directly to Bella Rosa, email mike@bellarosadevelopments.ca or call 403-588-0407.
