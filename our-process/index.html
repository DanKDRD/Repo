Our process

A Clearer Path from Idea to Completed Work

Every project is different, but the same practical sequence keeps decisions visible and gives each conversation a useful purpose.

Seven practical stages

Make the next decision at the right time.

The process begins with the property, the problem to solve, the desired result, and the information already available. Scope, budget, design, and construction decisions become more specific as the project moves forward.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    Tell Mike what you are considering, where the property is, and what is driving the project.

  2. 02

    Site walk-through

    Look at the space or lot together and identify the practical questions early.

  3. 03

    Scope & budget

    Discuss priorities, constraints, budget range, and where decisions may affect cost.

  4. 04

    Design & feasibility

    Review the layout, construction approach, and information needed before pricing.

  5. 05

    Quote & planning

    Turn the agreed direction into a defined scope and workable project plan.

  6. 06

    Build coordination

    Coordinate the work, trades, details, and homeowner decisions through construction.

  7. 07

    Final walk-through

    Review the completed work together and identify anything that still needs attention.

Before pricing

Useful information reduces avoidable assumptions.

Bring the project address, photographs, available plans, priorities, budget range, preferred timing, and any known site or building constraints.

If something is not known yet, identify it openly. The next step may be a site visit, additional design information, municipal confirmation, or a narrower scope before pricing can be useful.
