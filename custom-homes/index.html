A practical approach

A custom build should begin with the way you want to live

Bellarosa works with homeowners who want to make deliberate choices about the layout, structure, finishes, and function of a new home. The goal is not to push you toward a standard package. It is to understand what matters, surface practical constraints, and build a clear path from early ideas to construction.

Mike’s background spans concrete foundations, framing, carpentry, millwork, drywall, and finishing. That range helps connect design decisions to the way a home will actually be built.

Experience applied early

Why owner-led building matters

A custom home includes hundreds of connected decisions. When the person guiding the project understands the work behind the drawings, concerns can be raised before they are hidden behind drywall or locked into a finish selection.

That does not remove every surprise from construction. It creates a more useful conversation about feasibility, sequence, and trade-offs while there is still time to make a good decision.

Prepare for a useful first meeting

Useful decisions to bring to the first conversation

You do not need every answer. A clear starting point helps identify the questions that should be answered next.

  • Where you plan to build and what is known about the lot
  • The spaces your household needs now and in the next several years
  • Examples of layouts, materials, or details you respond to
  • A realistic budget range and financing stage
  • Your ideal timing and any hard deadlines
  • Which professionals, plans, surveys, or site information are already in place

Common questions

Before you get started.

Do you build custom homes in Red Deer and Central Alberta?+

Bellarosa’s current website describes custom-home work in Red Deer and Central Alberta. Confirm the property location and current service range during the first conversation.

Can Bellarosa help with an acreage build?+

Acreage and custom-lot planning can be discussed early, including access, services, orientation, and foundation considerations. The exact location remains subject to owner confirmation.

Do I need finished plans before making contact?+

No. An early conversation can help identify what information is needed before plans and pricing move forward.

What should I know before starting?+

Start with a realistic budget range, your site information, your must-have spaces, and the decisions you are willing to make. Clear priorities make later design conversations more productive.

What warranty and builder-licence requirements apply?+

Current builder-licence and new-home warranty details must be confirmed directly with Bellarosa before a project proceeds.
